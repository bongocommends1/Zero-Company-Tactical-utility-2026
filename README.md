## 🎮 About the Game

STAR WARS Zero Company is a single-player, turn-based tactics game released on August 27, 2026. It was developed by Bit Reactor in collaboration with Respawn Entertainment and Lucasfilm Games. Set during the twilight of the Clone Wars, the game follows former Republic officer Hawks as he leads an unconventional squad against the Infinite Coil.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive utility for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This utility enhances the single-player tactical experience by managing operator health, action points, Advantage points, mission resources, injuries, and squad progression. It is designed around the game's turn-based combat, operator abilities, base operations, and strategic campaign systems.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Operators use action points to move across the battlefield and take cover. Enable Unlimited Action Points before moving to freely reposition the squad, reach objectives, or set up firing positions. Movement remains governed by the game's map, range, cover, and line-of-sight rules.

### Tools & Inventory

Use resource options when managing campaign equipment, loadouts, and squad development. Credits support applicable campaign activities, while operator customization remains handled through the game's normal loadout and appearance systems.

### World & Environment

The Den serves as Zero Company's base of operations, where Hawks selects missions and operations from the Holotable. Intel is spent on non-combat assignments that can provide items, credits, and Influence. Enable Unlimited Intel or Influence before confirming an operation when testing different strategic paths.

### Combat & Weapons

Each operator normally receives three action points per turn. Use Unlimited Health to protect the squad, Unlimited Advantage to activate special actions, and No Injuries to reduce the consequences of being downed. These options work alongside attacks, overwatch, rally actions, assists, cover, and squad combat synergies.
